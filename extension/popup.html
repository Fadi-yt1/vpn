VLESS / Trojan / VMess servers need a small free helper on your computer (one-time setup, about 1 minute). It runs Xray-core and the extension controls it for you.

Windows – in PowerShell, from the downloaded helper folder:

powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File install_windows.ps1

macOS / Linux – in Terminal:

bash install.sh

Then click “Recheck”. See README for details.
